How DIME changes its nightly SOC-loss budget

During playback, cards and table summarise completed nights and days.

NightStart SOCNext day gainDIME chosen lossEnd-of-night SOCNext night-start SOCWhy this decision?
For each night: desired loss = current dusk SOC + max(0, predicted daylight gain − conservative haircut) − desired next-dusk SOC. The result is bounded by the minimum lighting-service loss, maximum adjustable loss and (current SOC − reserve − buffer). When the minimum service loss alone breaches reserve, the model flags an infeasible conflict rather than claiming a safe command. This demonstrates the logic, not a verified battery-energy or dimming model.
